Twice a day, the ocean leaves a small world behind.

Tide pools are rock basins that hold seawater when the tide goes out. Every animal in them has to survive heat, waves, and being stranded.

Meet the animals

The shore has three zones

Where an animal lives depends on how long it can go without water. Look for the same layers on any rocky coast.

High zone

Covered only by the highest tides. Barnacles and periwinkle snails seal up to stay moist.

Middle zone

Covered and uncovered every day. Mussels and hermit crabs fill the pools here.

Low zone

Underwater almost all the time. Sea stars, anemones, and urchins live here, and you only see them at the lowest tides.
