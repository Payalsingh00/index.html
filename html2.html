Plan your visit

A good trip starts with the tide chart, not the beach.

Before you go

  • Check a tide chart and arrive one hour before low tide. A low tide of 0.5 feet or below uncovers the most life.
  • Wear shoes with grip. Wet rock and seaweed are slippery.
  • Never turn your back on the ocean. Waves can surge past the waterline.
  • Step on bare rock, not on animals.
  • Touch gently with one wet finger, or just watch.
  • Put every rock back where you found it, since animals live underneath.
  • Leave shells, stars, and urchins in place. Even empty shells may be a hermit crab’s future home.
